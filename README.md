# Antonio Rojas Castro

I am a **digital humanities and research data specialist** working at the intersection of humanities scholarship, digital collections and research infrastructures. My work focuses on the organisation, analysis and documentation of structured textual data, particularly historical and literary corpora.

Over the past decade, I have contributed to international research projects developing digital scholarly editions, data models and reproducible research workflows. My experience includes text collection curation, metadata modelling, computational analysis and research data documentation for collaborative and Open Science environments.

## Areas of work

- **Digital scholarly editing and collections:** creating, encoding and curating texts using XML/TEI.
- **Research data and metadata:** modelling, enriching and documenting structured humanities data.
- **Computational analysis:** exploring historical and literary corpora with Python and Jupyter.
- **Research infrastructures and Open Science:** developing reproducible workflows, documentation and training resources.
- **Teaching:** digital humanities and digital scholarly editing, including MA thesis supervision at the Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (UNIR).

## Selected projects

- **[HallerNet](https://hallernet.org/):** work on digital scholarly editions and research data.
- **[Soledades](http://soledades.uni-koeln.de/#/critical?d=doc_1&e=critical):** text edition with apparatus.
- **[CalDraCor](https://dfg-spp-cls.github.io/projects_en/2020/01/24/TP-Tracing_Regularities_in_Calderon/):** curation and analysis of a corpus of more than 200 Spanish plays.
- **[DraCorOS](https://dracor-org.github.io/dracor-textbook/index.html):** research data workflows, training resources, documentation and Open Science practices at Freie Universität Berlin.

I have held research positions at the **Cologne Center for eHumanities**, the **Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities** and the **University of Tübingen**.
